Informed Consent for Participation in Educational Simulation

Study ID# BMS501-MMFS-2026

You are invited to participate in an educational outbreak simulation being conducted at Murdoch University's School of Medical, Molecular and Forensic Sciences (MMFS). This simulation supports training for postgraduate students in outbreak investigation and public health response.

Purpose: Your participation helps create realistic outbreak data for students learning outbreak investigation skills. This is an educational exercise, not a research study.

If you agree to participate:

  • You will take part in a simulated outbreak game that will run for one week (18-21 May 2026).
  • The app will show you a simulated health status on its main screen. This status may change depending on the spread of the virtual pathogen during the game.
  • You will be asked to make daily choices about virtual protective behaviours. These choices will not affect your daily activities but will influence the simulation.
  • You can scan QR codes around Building 240 and Building 250 courtyards to access virtual protective measures such as masks or vaccines for your avatar.
  • You will interact with other participants through proximity when scanning QR codes around campus.

Privacy: No identifiable information (like your name, email, or location) will be collected. Instead, your app will be assigned a random code (UUID) that cannot be traced back to you. Your responses and interaction data will be stored securely and used only for educational purposes within MMFS. Academic staff may review your data for educational feedback purposes.

Participation: Your participation is voluntary. You are free to decline or withdraw at any time by selecting the "Leave Game" option in the app, which will also delete your data from the system.

Contact: If you have any questions about this simulation, you can contact the unit coordinator (A/Prof Charlotte Oskam) at BMS501@murdoch.edu.au.

Thank you for considering participation in this educational simulation.
