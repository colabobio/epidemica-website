🧬 The Epigame @ VinUni: The Sequencing Challenge

The Epigame @ VinUni is heading into its final days! ⏱️

But the Sequencing Challenge is still live, and we want to make sure everyone has a fair shot at unlocking the final mystery. If you're wondering how to crack the code, here are some more hints:

1. Acquire the Tool

The sequencing tool is available for free in the shop. However, it will only appear in the shop if your avatar is currently sick.

2. Understand the FASTA Format

Once used, the tool generates a fragment of the pathogen's genome in FASTA format. It will look something like this:

>total_seq_len:400 gcaatctagtaggattacctaataatatttgcctgcaaaagacatctaatcagatattgaagccaaagctgatttcatac

The Header: The line starting with > tells you the total length of the full genome, which is 400 nucleotides.

The Sequence: The letters (gcaa...) represent the specific nucleotides in your fragment.

3. Export Your Data

To get this fragment off your phone for analysis, use the "Share To Analyze" button. You can send it to yourself or your team via email, cloud storage, or messaging apps.

4. The Power of Collaboration 🤝

Here is the catch: your fragment is only about 80 nucleotides long and is pulled from a random location in the genome. To reconstruct the full sequence, you must collaborate with other players!

5. Assemble the Puzzle

Since fragments are generated from random positions, they will overlap. Think of it like shredding a long sentence into small strips of paper — you can reconstruct the original message by finding where the words overlap.

Goal: Gather enough fragments from different players and find a way to align and combine them into one continuous sequence.

6. Claim Your Reward

Once you believe you have the full sequence:

  1. Go to SEQUENCING.EPIDEMICA.INFO.
  2. Enter the game join code: vinuepi26.
  3. Paste your reconstructed sequence.

But note that you will be able to paste your reconstructed sequence only when there are 3 days or fewer remaining of the epigame. So you still have time to work on the puzzle 😀

If your sequence is at least 90% accurate, you will unlock a special reward QR code!

7. More to Come

Don't worry if you find the sequencing tough — we have a few more surprises in store before the Epigame concludes.

Thank you for your continued participation and good luck, researchers!
