The Epigame @ VinUni: Day 5 🔥🔥🔥

This notice is to remind you of the 🧬 SEQUENCING CHALLENGE 🧬 in the epigame. This is optional but if you take up the challenge, you can unlock a special final reward!

Those players who get sick from the disease can use the sequencing item from the shop (which is free) to get a piece of the virus genome. The fragment comes in a text format called FASTA used in bioinformatics.

Since each player only has one fragemnt, you need to collaborate with others to assemble the complete genome. You can share the FASTA files with your teammates in any way you want.

You can find the solution by looking at how these fragments overlap and the form the full sequence. Not easy, but it is a challenge!

If you think you have found the solution, you can upload it to SEQUENCING.EPIDEMICA.INFO
